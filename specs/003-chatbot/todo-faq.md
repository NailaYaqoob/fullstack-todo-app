# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I add a task?
Click on the "Add Task" button and enter your task details.

## Can I edit a task?
Yes, click on the edit icon next to the task.

## How do I delete a task?
Click the delete button or swipe left on mobile.

## Is my data محفوظ (secure)?
Yes, all data is encrypted and stored securely.

## Can I recover deleted tasks?
No, deleted tasks cannot be recovered.
